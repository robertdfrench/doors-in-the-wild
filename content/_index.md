---
title: "Doors in the Wild"
---
*An annotated survey of naturally occuring door clients, server
procedures, calling conventions of the same, error handling practices,
various idiosyncrasies, and interesting counter-examples drawn
from real applications in both the kernel and userland code,
having been gathered, collected, and refined by Robert French.*

See [Revolving Doors][1] for a guided introduction.

[1]: https://github.com/robertdfrench/revolving-doors
