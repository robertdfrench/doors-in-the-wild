*A nice and accurate survey of naturally occuring door clients,
server procedures, calling conventions of the same, error
handling practices, various idiosyncrasies, and interesting
counter-examples drawn from real applications in both the kernel
and userland code, having been gathered, collected, and refined
by [Robert D. French](https://robertdfrench.com).*
